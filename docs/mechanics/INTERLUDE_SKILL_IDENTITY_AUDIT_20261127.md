## Priorities and design constraints

1. Distinguish root (no movement, can attack), slow (less movement), stun/paralysis (lost actions), sleep (breaks on damage), silence (no spells, not all physical attacks), and cooldown delay. Existing stagger and slow are not interchangeable. Separate physical CON and magical MEN resistance, bounded duration and shared immunity to prevent stun-lock. Effects must consume an action and be visible in trace.
2. Add anti-heal and magical/physical vulnerability, not just universal outgoing damage weakening. Curse Gloom is magical defence reduction; Curse Disease is healing reduction; Seal of Scourge concerns natural HP recovery, not all healing. No MP system: do not literally port Mana Burn, Recharge or Magical Backfire; only consider distinct cooldown-related equivalents when justified and approved.
3. Preserve tank-first attacks and healing tank priority. Control target selection on actual threats (closing melee, healer about to act, spell caster) is a proposal requiring approval, not a silent change to aggro. Resist/cleanse, short nonrefreshable durations and cooldowns are counterplay; avoid applying every effect to tank forever.
4. Buffs distinguish weapon/casting cadence, critical rate, resistance and movement, with nonstacking categories and actual target needs. Pet/cubic skills should begin as bounded companion effects unless separate actors approved. Physical and magic support identity differs, including Feline Queen versus Seraphim versus Nightshade.
5. Preserve one passive plus two automatic cooldown skills initially; consider replacing/combining current skills, not adding every listed skill. Validate max-only varied balanced teams in all five zones, paired seeds, damage, control uptime, healing denied, actual ability use, timeouts, deaths, and class-specific utility. No scripted wins, no forced roster. Exact cooldowns, ranges, target rules and status chances await owner decision.
