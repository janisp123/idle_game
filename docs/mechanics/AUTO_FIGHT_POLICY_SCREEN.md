3/1/10 is a reasonable next candidate, not final balance: highest mean income online and locked offline among these six, lowest loss rate, but loses S-tier income when unlucky retreat happens before logout; 10 victories costs 33m20s of active bout time. After one hour from NG its mean online income is 51.48%, offline expectation 83.79%; this highlights cold-start search costs. A single defeat does not necessarily indicate the tier is unprofitable. Need compare alternative cooldowns, including shorter cooldown at stable/previously confirmed tier, and saved-stage starts. The 1/1/0 case is a negative result: too much oscillation. Blocked-tier state in the screen stores only the most recent retreat target. Not a proposed save schema. Empirical probabilities limited to sampled opponents/builds; no actual temporal opponent correlations, CAP-001, gear changes, novice profiles, weekly trajectories, XP or wallet transactions modeled. Do not use these numbers as six-month economy forecasts or claim offline engine parity.

## Corrected implementation contract
- Preserve eight NG/F/E/D/C/B/A/S difficulty tiers, five weapon classes and existing arena terrains. Remove manual Fighting difficulty selector only; other activity difficulties unchanged. No clicks/active abilities, no apprentice unlock requirement. New character starts NG automatically.
- Online visible live fight completion updates policy exactly once after the completed bout; wins and defeats/double-deaths from shared resolver, no artificial level/gear gates. Provisional 3 consecutive wins, 1 loss, 10 wins retry, pending confirmation from additional tests. Highest defeated stage can be recorded as history separate from current stage.
- OFFLINE: lock saved committed difficulty, counters, retry state and highest defeated stage across absence. No difficulty advance OR retreat, even if all fights win/lose. Offline rewards use that grade, existing frozen build, resurrection time, XP cap, queue transitions and bounded 256-bout schedule. Offline fights do not count toward online streak or record. On return settle offline first; resume policy on subsequent genuinely live completion. Gear/stat changes take effect at normal bout boundary, not retroactively. Clarify hidden tab, reload, background throttling and partial duel crossings; conservative policy is no advancement on catch-up or hidden time and no duplicate credit. Do not allow arbitrary stage mutation via old Fighting setDifficulty path.
- Production map: combat.mjs seeded physical duel and snapshot validation; arena-wallet.mjs fixed-tier payouts and partial downtime; engine.mjs desiredArenaPlayer, settleArenaBuild, settleWork cap binary probes, settle, setDifficulty, activity transitions; app.mjs visibility, tick, persistence, difficulty UI; existing simulator and systems lab. Keep fixed-tier bulk offline settlement; online path split only at committed bout boundaries, preserve frozen in-flight player and payout cursor, no new roll on reload. Do not mutate progress in discarded cap probes. Existing grade purchase and expertise rules unchanged.
- Next: run production-linked policy simulations using current A3 source, fresh/weak/strong/specialization-switched and all 15 milestone builds, live session lengths and daily/weekly returners; compare retries and stage start policies. Then implement pure policy with testable validated state and online-only explicit settlement mode, followed by UI, six-month economy and source/CI/browser verification.
