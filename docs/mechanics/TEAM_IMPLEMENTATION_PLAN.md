## Approved direction
Two gladiators from the beginning, controlled by one manager. Identical shared base STR/DEX/END and Fighting skill; independent weapon, armour, equipment condition, practice, and owned upgrade tracks. Player may freely switch either loadout; progress stays with its owner and item/style. Assistant automatically upgrades selected gear for both. Shared treasury; no shared item ownership or expertise. Both attack automatically in symmetrical 2v2 combat. The surviving member continues fighting. No manual combat actions, artificial class locks or guaranteed tank aggro. Use existing five weapons and four armour slots. "Enchant" here means the current 32-stage quality/grade upgrades, not resurrected historical scroll enchantment.
