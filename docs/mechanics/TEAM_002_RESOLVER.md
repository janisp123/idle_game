# TEAM-002 — modular roster combat and Teamwork pilot

Owner approved implementation 2026-11-26, details adjustable later; explicit requirement for modular code and bulk team testing. Baseline d6a56fae2e4be62684046844c6253fbdf4dbd169, draft PR #188; no live team integration yet.

## Modules
- `physical-actions.mjs`: existing physical fighter creation, strike, damage, protection, durability, critical/guard/status and stat primitives extracted from `physical-duel.mjs`. Single-duel controller calls the same strike. Optional onBleed callback records source for team combat. No damage formula changed. Existing stat breakdown still uses same material factors.
- `team-combat.mjs`: pure bounded roster resolver, 1..6 per side; intended playable roster remains two per side. All actors have stable side/index IDs and separate position, HP, recovery, ammo, gear and action count. 9 columns, height max roster size, initial symmetric front/rear positions (bow behind melee), Manhattan tile gap minus 1 = distance, existing weapon ranges. Occupied living tiles block movement, bounded breadth-first search to reachable attack tiles, finite bow retreats. Initiative DEX + mobility/10 + uniform 0..10. No manual tactics or artificial mandatory aggro. Target score favors reachable/near opponents; coordinated decisions also favor allies' current targets and injured opponents (focus 2 per ally, health 1.5). Independent seeded decisions stream. Adjacent living shield bearer with intact shield, no stagger/open guard and at least ally HP fraction can swap to intercept melee attack once/round, recovery at least 1. No guaranteed interception, no ranged interception. Original shield block/armour apply; no new direct damage modifier. Bleed persists from original source, dead actors cannot initiate new actions. Surviving ally continues. Up to 32 rounds. Elimination, otherwise larger remaining team HP fraction with inflicted damage wins; tie/no-damage double death. Return pre-verdict survivor HP, participation and full event trace; execution/resurrection/reward integration remains open.
- `teamwork.mjs`: independent shared skill of the current fixed roster, not shared weapon/armour mastery. Provisional 1..50, one XP per settled team bout, next bar 100×current level, capped; login activates at most one level and clears XP. At max no XP. Coordination probability linear 15% at level 1 to 85% at 50. Used for target coordination and interception, no direct damage boost. Only pure helper implemented; live cap/settlement, XP eligibility on losses and roster-change rules need later design. No additional selection UI.
- `tools/simulation/team-combat.mjs`: imports the exact candidate resolver, real snapshot builder and NPC generator. `teamCombat` registered in `tools/system-lab.mjs`; accepts 1..100,000 bouts, seed, grade, each roster build, teamwork levels, optional fixed opponent roster and arena, up to ten traces. Reports team verdicts, arena split, actions, damage, pre-verdict survival, interceptions. Developer-only adapter, no public test/admin bypass.
