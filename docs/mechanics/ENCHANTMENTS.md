# Glory Scrolls and slot enchanting — minimal alpha (GLORY-001)

This module implements the Owner's simple foundation: **a real Fighting victory may award a Glory Scroll (50%) only while Fighting can still earn XP; one scroll gives a 50% chance to increase a chosen equipment-slot enchantment by +1; failure resets that one slot to +0**. Existing 3d6 battle formulas, Skill Master, resource spoils, and limitless offline working time remain intact.

## Rewards

- `glory_scroll` is an ordinary persisted inventory count with the visible label **Glory Scrolls**; it is not a currency, generated gear item, recipe material, purchase or Skill Master tribute. Other activities never produce it. No per-kill resources/XP are fabricated.
- The game already resolves a real opponent's `victory`/`defeat`/`stalemate` using deterministic 3d6 duels. Only **fully completed player victories** are eligible; defeating an enemy by collapse or winning by the final judge both count. Each eligible victory gets one independent **50%** chance of **one** Glory Scroll. Defeats and draws award none. The drop check uses a separate deterministic salt from bout and frozen session seed, never advancing/changing combat dice.
- Eligible bouts must **begin while the current Fighting XP bar can earn XP**, before the Skill Master's one-level cap. The XP window is frozen at the beginning of a combat session from `xpToNextLevel(level)-earnedXP`, current activated Fighting efficiency, nominal Fighting XP per completed work cycle, and unfinished current-cycle progress. A bout that started with available XP may award its scroll after its final verdict, even if the XP bar filled during the fight. Once the XP window is exhausted, subsequent victories award **nothing**, until the player actually claims another Fighting level at the Skill Master and a new session begins. An XP-full skill at the beginning of a session has no eligible bouts.
- The XP window uses the existing nominal XP rate, **not a new per-duel XP system**. The game's ±10% batch XP variation may shift the exact cap timestamp slightly; snapshot eligibility is the deliberately simple provisional alpha rule. We don't pretend a new victory earned XP individually, nor interpret skill-level eligibility from time-earned attributes.
- A saved optional `combatSession.gloryCursor` prevents double-paying completed bouts, with `gloryEligibleUntilMs` as its frozen window. Older saved sessions without this tracker begin paying from **the next fresh bout only**, never inventing historical winners after an old migration. The reward clock is persisted locally, alongside the existing original combat snapshot.
- **Offline works:** settle from an arbitrarily long absence only reconstructs the *completed bouts eligible within the finite XP window* and checks their **actual deterministic outcomes**. It does not replay 20 years of ineligible defeats/victories, fabricate aggregate kills, silently cap offline hours or reward after the XP bar fills. Computational work depends on how many bouts fit before the XP cap (and could be nonconstant at an extreme, manually activated Fighting rank), **not** the length of absence. Exact roll-free aggregate offline Fighting remains GAME-012 and is **not** implemented by this module.

## Enchanting

- The gear drawer remains collapsed until a player opens **EQUIPMENT**. A compact Glory count and two actions enchant either the currently selected **weapon slot** or the currently selected **individual armour slot** among eleven. No generated item instances, random gear names, or database.
- Every attempt **spends exactly one Glory Scroll**, after explicit confirmation. Independent random roll `r<0.5`: **success** increases only that slot's enchant rank by 1; **failure** resets only that slot's rank **to zero**. Other equipment slots, skill levels, permanent attributes, resources, and existing gear choices are untouched.
- **Weapon +N** adds **+N raw physical attack** before existing weapon-skill damage multipliers and armour reduction (1 point per rank). **Armour +N** from each of the eleven body slots adds to a single **flat physical defense** total (1 point per rank) applied only after a landed physical blow has passed the 3d6 defense and normal armour reduction; at least 1 HP damage remains. The enchant has **no extra 3d6 bonus**, no healing, no magical effect, no mobility change. This is not yet final PvP balance.
- Maximum rank **+20 per slot**, provisional and deliberately rare (twenty 50/50 successes in a row). This is a tunable alpha safety bound to avoid numerical domination/overflow; future owner-approved balancing can change it with proper existing-save migration.
- Enchantments are attached to **equipment slots**, not items. An enchanted left glove remains that slot's +N defense even after switching Leather/Plate/Mage Robe/None. The `weapon` slot remains +N attack across the two existing combat styles. A failed attempt on another slot never resets it. Existing equip restrictions (STR for Plate/DEX for Leather/WIL for Mage) still apply to equipping gear; **additional enchant-rank stat requirements** are future Owner decisions (do not invent them silently).
- Optional V7 `enchantments` field has the 12 known bounded integer ranks; an old save without it gains twelve +0 ranks without losing stats, XP, progress, resources, equipment, queues or frozen duel state. Unknown fields, negatives and over-cap ranks are rejected as malformed; scroll count is an optional inventory key in older saves and defaults to 0. Settle prior work under old effective enchants before each attempt; on actual rank change in ongoing Fighting, reset just its duel for fresh frozen attack/defense power.
