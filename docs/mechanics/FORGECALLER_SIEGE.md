# FORGECALLER-SIEGE-001 — Clan siege engineer

Owner direction, 2026-11-28: make Forgecaller really good at clan PvP and sieges. This is an approved class identity for future clan warfare, not approval to launch shared multiplayer or fixed numerical coefficients.

## Current evidence

Forgecaller is a Dwarf Artisan, physical summoner using a hammer and plate, with Construct Bond, Construct Strike and Siege Pulse. V10 class substitution screen (#221) used 3700 productive hours, S Masterwork, class and stance level 50, 8 environments and three enemy compositions. Forgecaller teams won 33/96; the siege roster won 654/960, its Blademaster replacement 780/960. In the direct paired comparison the Forgecaller roster won 20/64. These are context-specific tests, not proof of uselessness. No clan warfare exists in current local alpha; the siege team in that test was an ordinary arena composition.

## Intended specialization

Forgecaller is a mechanical siege engineer, not a magic caster. Preserve hammer and plate, independent class mastery and gear progression. In clan PvP give him concrete battlefield jobs rather than an unconditional damage bonus for the mode:

- Construct Bond: sustained mechanical construct support for coordinated assaults; constructs have finite durability, are targetable and can be destroyed or interrupted. Reuse the existing bounded deployment/expiry lifecycle as far as suitable.
- Construct Strike: focus on hostile barricades, gates, siege engines and defensive structures; pressure shield walls and protected formations without bypassing all mitigation.
- Siege Pulse: deliberate, telegraphed area pressure against clustered defenders, with meaningful setup and recovery; effective against fortifications and coordinated formations. Multiple pulses/constructs must have bounded stacking.
- In defense, constructs help contest chokepoints and enemy siege machinery. In open, mobile combat, lack of mobility, setup time and vulnerability to flanking or ranged disruption are natural weaknesses.

Do not apply a blanket clan-size multiplier, automatic win, forced class quotas, or buff ordinary 7v7 arenas merely because the group is called a clan. No new currency, consumable or manual daily task is required. Real shared clans, player authentication, server-authoritative objectives, positioning, structure health, targeting and warfare rewards remain separately gated. Exact siege geometry, army sizes, coefficients and construct behavior are future design/calibration decisions; do not assume these systems exist today.

## Acceptance when clan warfare is implemented

- Same-investment mirrored tests using production resolver, max six-month benchmark, S Masterwork, class/stance/teamwork 50; separately compare lower progression for healthy scaling.
- Compare Forgecaller versus same-role and melee replacements in multiple credible offensive and defensive clan rosters, with fortifications, open ground, chokepoints, anti-siege counterplay and multiple seeds; report objective contribution, structure damage, time to breach, survival, control and team outcomes.
- Forgecaller should be an exceptional choice for actual fortified assaults and siege defense, and materially improve some competitive clan rosters. No expectation of dominance on every map. Ordinary arena results should stay unchanged absent explicit approval for arena tuning.
- Test multiple engineers for bounded stacking and counterplay, both sides equally, and no mandatory numerical class quota. Require reliable deterministic replay, server ownership and authoritative validation before public clan PvP.
- Test offline scheduling/settlement, no double rewards or duplicate construct effects, no retroactive changes to already frozen arena or raid encounters. Record measured results and review before release.

Current state: design only. Blocked on future real clan PvP/siege implementation. No runtime changes.
