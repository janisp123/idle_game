# GEAR-RESEARCH-001 — Arena difficulty and equipment grade (2026-11-25, chat)

Research only, baseline `01362378d8aefcec3aa1fa4bb73116d9457237c3`. Uses proposed incremental expertise/grade bonuses and 24+3g+12q durability, not deployed mechanics. Existing arena rewards, benchmark stats/skill, NPC encounter profiles, terrain selection and 200-second bouts retained. No gameplay, economy, save, access-gate or deployment changes.

## Method

Actual pinned combat.startCombatSession, combat.combatAtTime, NPC makeOpponent, terrain selection, candidate in-memory smith/physical-duel formulas and actual arenaReward. Test two independent fixed session seeds 20261125 and 1298729031, first 32 bouts each, 64 bouts per build/cell. Outcome counts include draws; no outcome filtering. Coin/hour = wins / bouts × current difficulty prize × 18 (200 seconds per bout, recovery included); assumes continuous available fighting time, not daily XP-capped income. No report of actual human behaviour. All profiles use five weapons × three uniform four-piece armour families except initial unarmoured stress. Same deterministic seeds across comparisons. Each 8×8 matrix 61,440 bouts; starter 2,560. Separately reconstruct 60 saved day30/90/150/180 full-credit daily progression snapshots from earlier economy JSON (15 builds × 4 milestones), test all eight arenas, 30,720 additional bouts. Total 217,600 duel executions. Single initial harness binding collision corrected before successful execution; initial all-file snapshot retrieval exceeded tool-call limit, then split into two batches. Harness bodies executed in isolated JS; standalone Node CLI and CI not independently verified.

Run from repo root:
- `node tools/gear-difficulty-study.mjs freshPoor` (other profile IDs below, omit to run all).
- `node tools/gear-difficulty-trajectories.mjs` (reads prior saved full-credit trajectories; optional paths for subset).
- Raw output links below include per-build wins/defeats/draws and coin rates. These are sampled matchups, not guarantees.

Profiles: freshPoor = all gear Poor, same stats and skill as its gear-grade arena benchmark, expertise at previous grade threshold (NG zero); masteredMW = all gear Masterwork, grade-matched stats/skill, full expertise; overlevelledMW = same gear but S benchmark stats 144/144/144 skill50 at every grade, fully trained; starterUnarmoured = NG Poor weapons, no armour, stats10/skill1. NPCs generated by live logic: NG Poor, later Common, fully trained, stat variation and weighted role/armour selection. No hard grade requirement proposed or added.

Normal progression: zero of 120 build/grade combinations in freshPoor and zero of 120 in masteredMW earn maximal coins/hour above their gear grade. Adjacent higher arena win rates typically below 1% fresh, up to 2.2% aggregate mastered; two grades higher zero observed for these profiles. No profitable upward skipping at matching benchmark stats. Fresh Poor own-grade average win rate ~31–38% at F–S; mastered ~63–64%, so one grade below often earns more. Starter unarmoured wins 45.6% NG, 0.3% F, none above; approximately 99 coins/hour at NG. Not an invincibility requirement: high attributes are meant to matter.

Overlevelled stress: 79/120 combinations prefer an arena above gear grade, 59/120 prefer ≥2 above. NG Masterwork with S stats wins 95.4% D, 62.5% C, 13.0% B, 3.0% A, 0.8% S (rounded). Highest mean income D about 824 coins/hour vs NG 216. This is a deliberately artificial stat/equipment mismatch, not a normal progression exploit demonstrated from a fresh account. It does show a strict gear-only separation claim would be false. S-grade arena still nearly impossible in NG gear even with S stats. Existing owner rule permits any arena attempt from day one; no arbitrary lock or reward penalty authorized. If owner wants universal grade dominance even with extreme stat differences, separate design approval and simulation needed.

The day180 S win rates span 17.19%–60.94%; A/S income ratios 1.25–4.28×. S NPC stats are 144 each and skill50, with variation; player snapshots have lower END and incomplete S mastery. Both higher same-grade NPC difficulty and prize curve contribute to relative income; effects not separated by this experiment. A prize is 122, S 158 (only 29.5% higher), and combat time is fixed. Do not blindly multiply S reward enough to satisfy weakest build: that could unbalance prices/progression.

## Recommendation and next action

Pass normal progression gear/difficulty separation and starter safety; fail strict universal gear gating under overlevelled stress; flag endgame coin incentive as unresolved. Keep open arena access and existing prices for now. Next compare NPC stat benchmark tuning (particularly late grades), mastery 3500h candidate if approved, and alternative reward curves using the same trajectories; prefer a solution that lets a fully prepared build earn more in its own arena without making fresh Poor automatically win or erasing tactical counters. Re-run six-month economy after any change. Final tactical counter balance and mixed equipment stress remain pending. No gameplay implementation until explicit approval.
