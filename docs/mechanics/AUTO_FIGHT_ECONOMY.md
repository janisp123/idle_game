## Interpretation and limitations
Recommend retaining 3/1/10, eight grades, current coin table and 4000h expertise. No observed economy blocker: all tested normal daily full-set specialists reached S within target horizon. Longer foreground can end on a retreat just before disconnect and reduce subsequent income; not guaranteed to be more profitable. This was a single seed per scenario, three selected builds for alternate cadences, deterministic shop and Master timing, not a statistically representative player population or proof every player can reach S in exactly six months. Daily zero foreground case is a theoretical limit, not a literal login with zero elapsed time. No change to skill cap or equipment gates, no claim that weekly play reaches S.
