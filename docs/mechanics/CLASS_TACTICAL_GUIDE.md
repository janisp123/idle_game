# Class tactical identities — 31 specializations

Owner-approved design direction: pronounced strengths and weaknesses by terrain. Tactical intentions, not guaranteed win rates. Actual equipment and cooldowns are sourced by the UI from the class registry. No forced team quotas. Summoner signatures are attacks and utility, not separate actors. Max-only balance validation.

## blademaster

Heavy twin-sword burst.

- Strong against: Exposed damage dealers; frontline pressure.
- Favourable terrain: Open Field, Walled Courtyard
- Weak against: Shielded tanks; ranged pressure while closing.
- Unfavourable terrain: Gatehouse Chamber: closing through confined space.

## linebreaker

Polearm reach and sweeping strikes against nearby enemies.

- Strong against: Crowded frontlines and closing melee.
- Favourable terrain: Narrow Bridge, Walled Courtyard
- Weak against: Enemies inside polearm reach; isolated ranged targets.
- Unfavourable terrain: Gatehouse Chamber: restricted reach.

## oathkeeper

Wards a nearby ally; intact shield intercepts arrows.

- Strong against: Archers and attacks on vulnerable allies.
- Favourable terrain: Narrow Bridge, Gatehouse Chamber
- Weak against: Magic and armour breakers; low offensive output.
- Unfavourable terrain: Open Field against mobile magic teams.

## grimwarden

Counterattack with temporary personal protection.

- Strong against: Physical attackers, especially archers.
- Favourable terrain: Narrow Bridge, Gatehouse Chamber
- Weak against: Magic and armour breakers; weak pursuit.
- Unfavourable terrain: Open Field against mobile casters.

## trickblade

Dagger feint opens enemy guard.

- Strong against: Padded-armour mages, exposed healers and archers at contact.
- Favourable terrain: Ruined Streets, Gatehouse Chamber
- Weak against: Plate armour and shields; must physically reach victims.
- Unfavourable terrain: Open Field: exposed while crossing distance.

## longshot

Powerful aimed bow strike.

- Strong against: Exposed casters and healers at distance.
- Favourable terrain: Open Field, clear Narrow Bridge sightlines
- Weak against: Shields, plate and opponents already in melee range.
- Unfavourable terrain: Ruined Streets, Gatehouse Chamber: obstructed shooting.

## flameweaver

Staff spells splash nearby enemies after a hit.

- Strong against: Clusters and heavily armoured tanks.
- Favourable terrain: Narrow Bridge, Gatehouse Chamber with protection
- Weak against: Fast daggers, clear arrow fire; fragile at contact.
- Unfavourable terrain: Open Field when exposed; confined fights without protection.

## gravebinder

Staff curse damages and weakens its target.

- Strong against: Heavy armour and dangerous damage dealers.
- Favourable terrain: Narrow Bridge, Walled Courtyard
- Weak against: Focused ranged fire and fast melee.
- Unfavourable terrain: Open Field when exposed; close contact without protection.

## beastcaller

Familiar-themed wand strike strengthens the caster; no separate pet actor.

- Strong against: Exposed opponents; sustained mixed-team pressure.
- Favourable terrain: Walled Courtyard, Open Field with protection
- Weak against: Focus fire and close melee; hybrid damage tradeoff.
- Unfavourable terrain: Gatehouse Chamber when exposed.

## lifekeeper

Strong direct healing for a wounded living ally; no revival.

- Strong against: Sustained damage against a protected frontline.
- Favourable terrain: Narrow Bridge, Walled Courtyard behind tanks
- Weak against: Burst and focus fire; limited offense.
- Unfavourable terrain: Open Field when exposed; Gatehouse Chamber without protection.

## runesage

Blesses a nearby ally with an offensive buff.

- Strong against: Strong allied damage dealers.
- Favourable terrain: Walled Courtyard, Narrow Bridge
- Weak against: Direct pressure, isolation, redundant buffs.
- Unfavourable terrain: Open Field when separated from allies.

## grovewarden

Wards a nearby ally and intercepts physical attacks.

- Strong against: Archers and pressure on vulnerable allies.
- Favourable terrain: Narrow Bridge, Gatehouse Chamber
- Weak against: Magic and armour breakers; isolated pursuit.
- Unfavourable terrain: Open Field against ranged magic.

## wardchanter

- Strong against: Sustained damage against clustered allies.
- Favourable terrain: Narrow Bridge, Gatehouse Chamber
- Weak against: Burst before wards activate; needs allied damage.
- Unfavourable terrain: Open Field if allies spread out.

## swiftblade

- Strong against: Exposed ranged fighters and casters at contact.
- Favourable terrain: Ruined Streets, Gatehouse Chamber
- Weak against: Heavy armour, shields and control.
- Unfavourable terrain: Open Field: must close before attacking.

## windbow

- Strong against: Exposed light-armour targets at range.
- Favourable terrain: Open Field, clear Narrow Bridge sightlines
- Weak against: Shields and close pursuit; cannot heal.
- Unfavourable terrain: Gatehouse Chamber, Ruined Streets.

## frostweaver

- Strong against: Closing melee and heavy armour.
- Favourable terrain: Narrow Bridge, Walled Courtyard behind allies
- Weak against: Focused archers and melee at contact.

- Unfavourable terrain: Gatehouse Chamber when exposed; open-field arrows.

## dreadguard
