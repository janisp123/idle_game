## Findings and implications

- Normal-profile daily fighting reaches level 59 with STR/DEX/END 141/141/131 at day 150 and level 68 with 156/155/143 at day 180. Old rough estimates omitted tier XP multipliers. These are conditional trajectories, not maxima.
- Normal hourly low-tier farming purchases an S Masterwork greatsword at 17h and a complete S Masterwork build at 40h; normal daily/weekly scenarios finish S gear at their day 2/7 visits. Alpha hourly reaches S weapon at 12h and set at 33h. Old cumulative S set cost 5,591 coins. This directly violates the long-term economy requirement.
- Normal daily player earns 4,754,640 coins in 180 days, but spends only 5,591 total. This is a baseline after exploiting cheap gear, not a forecast for the balanced economy; cannot price from it alone. The normal first-month low-tier farmer earns 152,532 coins even with early losses. Use the mathematical Non-grade maximum of 155,520 per 30 days (12 coins × 432 bouts/day × 30) as an initial anti-rush lower bound for an S-item price if rewards stay unchanged; higher tiers require their own bound/tests.
- Daily normal vs alpha levels at day 180: 68 vs 181. At day 30 hourly claims reach 20 vs 252. The accelerated testing profile must not dictate long-term progression. Weekly claims reach only 26 in both, because of the current single-level cap. Do not treat an exact calendar achievement as universal across return cadences.
- Mixed work gains more STR than pure Fighting but less DEX and fewer Fighting levels. Distinct builds remain useful. Expertise is unimplemented in this baseline.
- Most late bouts are trivial because old enemy stat benchmarks stop at ~38 while player attributes grow far beyond them; rework opponents jointly with gear scaling. Do not inflate armour universally or use arbitrary forced results.
- One sampled seed and fixed purchasing policy; these numbers are observed, not optimized earliest possible acquisition or statistical confidence. The normal engine profile was switched only inside a disposable copy. No player saves used.
