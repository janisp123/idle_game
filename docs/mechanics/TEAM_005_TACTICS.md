Recommendation: do not add third fighters/pets yet. First make existing choice actionable via an optional persistent arena/opponent preference (not per-bout clicking, not guaranteed matchup), accessible matchup/terrain breakdown from actual settled battles, and then separately test a small number of player-selected team behavior policies (protect ally, focus wounded, maintain range) with opportunity costs and active-only mastery; improve underperforming shield support via actual intercept/positioning evidence. These are candidate design suggestions only; require owner approval and separate controlled tests, no gameplay change. Validate mixed vs duplicate synergies and mastery/switching costs, intermediate and novice expertise, mixed armour, actual NPC/arena distributions, long-term economy and frozen offline parity before changing gameplay. No companion system needed to demonstrate tactical depth; more choices alone won't solve visibility/control.
