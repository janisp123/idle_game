## Durability findings
At S grade, identical global selected finalist (two aggressive plate hammer fighters) wins 37.4% against the held uniform enemy archetypes in all three models. Mean armour breaks per bout .003/0/0, condition lost 13.1/10.5/8.7%. S aggressive plate hammer + defensive padded greatsword finalist wins 41.8% in each model; armour breaks .013/.055/.088 per bout. S aggressive padded shield + defensive padded hammer wins 40.7/40.7/40.3%; breaks .012/.034/.080. NG aggressive/defensive leather bows global selected win 51.3/51.2/51.2%; D defensive leather bow + range leather hammer 45.4/45.4/45.6%. Durability changes actual breakage but rarely changes outcomes for these finalists. Strong model penalizes padded without demonstrating compensating tactical value. Moderate is a provisional candidate for clearer material identity, not an approved balance change.

## Diversity and PvP warning
S cross-play, baseline and moderate: dual aggressive plate hammers beat defensive leather bow + aggressive plate hammer 61.3%; defensive padded shield + tactical plate hammer beat dual aggressive plate hammers 78.8%; same shield/hammer loses to defensive plate bow + aggressive padded spear (31.3% win). Different opponents favour different teams. Only 80 bouts per directed pair, self-match win rates vary from 43.8% to 66.3%; directional reciprocal rates do not sum exactly to 100 due to seed/slot asymmetry. Treat as preliminary counter evidence, not equilibrium or definitive meta-free result.

Screen-selected leaders generalize poorly to wider held enemy population: e.g. S global selected 37.4% and S ruined streets candidate dual range bows 55.4% overall but 37.5% in that arena; D gatehouse candidate 55.4–55.6% overall but 31.3–32.1% locally; NG open field selected candidate 29.2% locally. Four screening seeds and restricted enemy pool are insufficient to rank builds reliably. Presence of weapon in winning mixed team does not prove its independent contribution. Some range-selected melee roles have no effective range mechanic. Do not claim all weapons viable or no meta from this run.

## Next

Test both Common and Masterwork; add mixed-slot armour and role contribution/counter checks. Examine why high-grade armour almost never breaks before death and fixed first-intact-slot wear; if meaningful armour attrition desired, test wear scaling by damage/grade or targeted material wear separately without changing health/resistance at the same time. Avoid simply boosting plate or weakening padded. Prioritize real competitive frontier (40–70% baseline win), actual offline daily earnings and independent progression constraints.
