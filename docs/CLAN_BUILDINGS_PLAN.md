# Clan buildings and weekly raid progression — implementation plan
2026-11-27 · CLAN-BUILDINGS-PLAN-001 · Draft for owner review · PR #224.
Baseline main b4624c1; local prototype #223 eb6be9e passed all seven checks, not merged. No gameplay changes authorized by this planning document.

## Owner requirements
One Clan tab; all five buildings start level 0. Barracks +5 slots each level; Bank up to +20% earned coins; Training Hall up to +20% Fighting XP; Armoury small increments across distinct useful combat statistics, around +20% each at completion; Portal unlocks weekly clan raids (50 levels), stronger permanent combat bonuses earned by defeating raids rather than buying them. No synchronous online participation required: each member clicks Join once per week. Full building progression takes more than a year even with a full clan. Clan membership is highly valuable for progression. Continue automatic every-tenth positive arena payout rule from #223; coin-only economy. Shared server authority is future work.

## Proposed configuration (not approved balance)
Base 10 members; Barracks 20 levels => 110 slots. Bank and Training Hall 20 levels each, +1 percentage point per level, level zero neutral. Armoury 100 levels, +1 percentage point to one stat each level, rotating five-level blocks HP, defence, damage, accuracy, healing; repeat four cycles => +20% each. Use bounded relative modifiers, not flat hit chance/immunity; scale healing without allowing immortal groups. Portal level = highest raid cleared, starts zero; raid 1 accessible immediately, 50 sequential clears. Raid milestones rotate same five stats, five-level blocks repeated twice; +3 percentage points per clear to its stat => +30% each at level 50. This is a candidate, not accepted tuning. Keep both sources independently visible; add bonuses within a stat (+50% max candidate before other effects), no compound clan stacking. Freeze combat bonus in battle session, preserve historical versions, both sides receive actual respective clan modifiers in future PvP. Training bonus applies only Fighting skill XP initially, not class/stance/teamwork; bank applies to ordinary arena coins before contribution. Exclude raid jackpots, alpha grants and historic wallet from donation. Maintain exact fractional coin carry, deterministic replay and CAP-001; no retroactive rewards, safe boundary on upgrades and membership.
 
## Economy and construction
Four coin-funded buildings total 160 levels. One construction queue, no speed-ups, early 20% of each building levels 1 day, middle 60% 3 days, final 20% 5 days => 480 calendar days minimum uninterrupted construction (not measured completion). Portal progression independent. Leader selects priorities; recommend automation starts next affordable authorized upgrade when queue free, treasury deducted once on start; no mandatory personal spending beyond automatic combat contribution. Upgrade price increases with level, fixed after simulation, not adjusted per player's income. Calibrate using actual arena reward/rank, offline XP cap, Bank bonus, member count and login frequency; simulate 10/25/50/75/110 members and multiple activity patterns including veterans at max benchmark, 100% active full clan, daily vs weekly logins. Reuse actual economy and round-level payout code; use real seeds and record inputs. Target realistic full active clan 16–24 months, hard construction floor 480 days; small clan viable early upgrades, no hard requirement to join full clan. Early benefits worthwhile, endgame costs progressively greater. No new currencies, real-money speedups, debt or resource warehouse. Compare gear pacing, rank and win rates with/without clan; no numerical prices accepted until simulations.

## Weekly clan raids
Server week Monday 00:00 UTC proposal. One clan attempt per week, no banking/retries; leader selects unlocked next raid, players opt in independently during signup. At scheduled cutoff freeze eligible members' seven-character rosters, stats, gear, stances and existing permanent bonuses, exclude nonmembers; offline accepted members participate. Resolve automatically using deterministic combat, no personal arena pause proposed. Victory permanently advances portal once, unlocks next raid and new buff for entire clan at next safe boundary; defeat no level loss, retry following week. 50 successful weeks minimum; harder tiers likely longer. No coins buy progress. No duplicate rewards, late join, rollover exploit or mid-raid build swapping. Show week countdown, level, expected difficulty/requirements, actual enrolled count, roster snapshot timestamp, result and unlocked bonus. Joining button once per week, idempotent; membership removal before cutoff cancels registration, after cutoff snapshot immutable (reward eligibility and clan transfer policy to review). For scalable many-player raids, use multiple seven-character groups against boss phases/crew, aggregate actual combat damage and support under one shared boss HP/phase state; compare against true multi-roster encounter reference, do not falsely represent separate victories as one raid. Calibrate lower raids for small groups, later bosses for many capable groups; roles/gear/terrain matter. Avoid fixed minimum attendance as sole difficulty or damage lottery. Exact raid mechanics, thresholds and names pending simulation. Test 50 tiers and varied rosters; combat duration bounded and deterministic; no HP-based victory for unresolved fights. Portal rewards stronger than Armoury, but validate long-term PvP gap and healer/defence stalemates.
