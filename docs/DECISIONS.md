| CHAR-001 | 2026-11-21 | Owner | **One character per account** when accounts are implemented; do not design progression around creating alternate characters. | RACE-001 | 2026-11-21 | Owner | Characters begin as **Human**. Human and Undead are **races/forms**, not classes or professions. No race choice permanently removes access to skills or forces creation of an alternate character. The earlier phrase "lock in Human" means a present preference, **not** an irreversible commitment. Exact costs, ritual scheduling/offline behavior, race effects, and interaction with the previously proposed monthly change limits are undecided. This records design only; no race gameplay or account system is authorized by this decision alone. | Active |
