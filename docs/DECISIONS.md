| SKILL-001 | 2026-11-21 | Owner | The player has a name but **no global character level or global XP level**. Each activity has independent skill XP and a skill level. Previous global XP cannot reliably be attributed to skills: migrate old saves, retain historical global XP invisibly, and start independent skills at level 1. Skill-based random higher-tier encounters/ore/recipes are an approved direction but require a separately implemented and tested reward-table phase. | Active |

| AUTO-MERGE-001 | 2026-11-21 | Owner | For work already explicitly agreed with chat/cw/codex, once a PR's final-head checks pass, scope and changed files are reviewed, there are no overlapping/unresolved conflicts, security/data-loss or quality issues, and no new owner decision is required, the agent may merge automatically without a separate “merge” request. Unclear/risky/new-scope work must stop for owner input. Preserve branch protection, QUAL-001, OFF-001, deployment verification and accurate recording of merges. Bare “Continue” remains read-only; unattended hourly research uses its existing narrow allowlist. | Active |

| CHAR-001 | 2026-11-21 | Owner | **One character per account** when accounts are implemented; do not design progression around creating alternate characters. Every character can eventually learn every activity/skill, without permanent class locks, mutually exclusive specializations, hard mastery caps, or forced resets/prestige. Advanced mastery should become very slow and remain a long-term personal pursuit, not a race or requirement to master everything. Ten years of patient mastery is a valid achievement, not a balance defect. This is a durable design rule, **not** an authorization to implement account infrastructure or change current progression formulas without a scoped task. | Active |

| RACE-001 | 2026-11-21 | Owner | Characters begin as **Human**. Human and Undead are **races/forms**, not classes or professions. Race transformations are **never permanent**: players may stay Human, become Undead through a resource-consuming ritual, and later reverse the change through another ritual. Each transformation should require resources and roughly **six real days** of ritual (duration provisional for balancing). No race choice permanently removes access to skills or forces creation of an alternate character. The earlier phrase "lock in Human" means a present preference, **not** an irreversible commitment. Exact costs, ritual scheduling/offline behavior, race effects, and interaction with the previously proposed monthly change limits are undecided. This records design only; no race gameplay or account system is authorized by this decision alone. | Active |
