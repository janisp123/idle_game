| SKILL-001 | 2026-11-21 | Owner | The player has a name but **no global character level or global XP level**. Each activity has independent skill XP and a skill level. | CHAR-001 | 2026-11-21 | Owner | **One character per account** when accounts are implemented; do not design progression around creating alternate characters. Every character can eventually learn every activity/skill, without permanent class locks, mutually exclusive specializations, hard mastery caps, or forced resets/prestige. Advanced mastery should become very slow and remain a long-term personal pursuit, not a race or requirement to master everything. Ten years of patient mastery is a valid achievement, not a balance defect. This is a durable design rule, **not** an authorization to implement account infrastructure or change current progression formulas without a scoped task. | Active |

| RACE-001 | 2026-11-21 | Owner | Characters begin as **Human**. Human and Undead are **races/forms**, not classes or professions. Race transformations are **never permanent**: players may stay Human, become Undead through a resource-consuming ritual, and later reverse the change through another ritual. Each transformation should require resources and roughly **six real days** of ritual (duration provisional for balancing). No race choice permanently removes access to skills or forces creation of an alternate character. The earlier phrase "lock in Human" means a present preference, **not** an irreversible commitment. Exact costs, ritual scheduling/offline behavior, race effects, and interaction with the previously proposed monthly change limits are undecided. This records design only; no race gameplay or account system is authorized by this decision alone. | Active |

| RACE-002 | 2026-11-21 | Owner | Race transformation ritual effort is **cumulative across multiple sessions**, not six consecutive days. A player may pause the ritual, do other activities for any length of time, and resume later from saved progress. No timeout, expiry, daily login requirement, or progress reset merely for switching activities or being absent. Approximately six days refers to total ritual work, not an uninterrupted calendar window. Whether the ritual runs automatically while offline when selected remains to be specified during implementation; do not imply passive progress while doing unrelated activities. | Active |

| COMBAT-SIMPLE-001 | 2026-11-21 | Owner | **Simplicity is a deliberate design priority.** Keep automated combat understandable, predictable enough to reason about, and inexpensive to simulate for long absences. Do not add layers of rules, statuses, decisions or bookkeeping just to make it look sophisticated. Favor a small core that produces enjoyable, meaningful progression; add complexity only for a demonstrated gameplay benefit. This is a design principle, **not** approval of unchosen formulas or specific features. | Active |
| DEATH-001 | 2026-11-21 | Owner | **Combat defeat costs productive time only.** The character resurrects automatically and returns to the same chosen hunting area/activity without requiring a login or manual restart. Death may consume time for resurrection and return travel, reducing net rewards, but must not subtract previously earned XP, inventory, currency, equipment or skill progress, damage gear, impose permanent punishment or stop an uncapped offline activity. Exact resurrection/travel durations, encounter mechanics and rates remain undecided. This is an approved design rule, **not** authorization to code the combat feature. | Active |
| ACT-SWITCH-001 | 2026-11-21 | Owner | **Manual activity changes are not instant.** Put a distinct **Start** button directly after each activity name; pressing Start on a different activity triggers a separate progress bar and sequential phase labels: **Finishing current activity**, **Moving to new location**, **Getting ready to start new activity**. The character finishes current work before changing location and preparing, and new activity begins only after the transition. Switching takes real time even if the browser is closed. Existing ZONE-001 five-minute cross-zone travel, OFF-001 frozen effective skill levels, and UI-TIME-001 no countdowns remain binding. Exact additional travel/preparation durations and handling of repeated Start presses are not yet chosen. This approves the UX behavior and design spec in `docs/ACTIVITY_SWITCH_SPEC.md`, **not a guessed numerical implementation or the deferred five-task queue**. | Active |
| QUEUE-001 | 2026-11-21 | Owner | **A linear queue of at most five activities (including the active and final activity); the final activity always runs forever.** Every previous activity may be configured through **Start → Do it for** to run 1–8 Hours or 1–8 Days (integer steps), then transitions automatically to the next activity, even offline for 20 years. The current work cycle finishes before handoff and travel/readiness occurs as separate transition phases; whether configured intervals count productive-only time or include switching time remains an open implementation choice. No automatic loop, offline cap, daily login, server cron job or end-of-queue idle. Resolve offline work in bounded batches per scheduled activity, with frozen effective levels (OFF-001), preserved inventory, and the ACT-SWITCH-001 three-phase transition; five-minute cross-zone travel remains. A static planning-duration selector is not a live timer, so UI-TIME-001 still prohibits countdowns and elapsed-time UI. This updates the earlier repeating/looping-queue brainstorming and ends that feature's prior deferral. Exact additional same-zone movement/preparation values and editing/cancellation during progress remain undecided; see `docs/ACTIVITY_QUEUE_SPEC.md`. No queued gameplay is implemented by this decision alone. | Active |
