# Open questions and distant-future ideas

**Status:** Brainstorming, not a task list. Updated 2026-11-20.

## Questions to settle before gameplay implementation
- Is the smallest prototype **stone mining + bunny hunting** (older direction), or **solo human versus several monster types** (newer direction)?
- Older notes describe **3d7 per encounter**; latest combat idea calls for **three six-sided skull dice per combat turn**. Work out exact odds, timing and rewards before coding.
- How do wind-up, dodge, casting interruption, recovery and battle-time scheduling interact without creating very long ordinary fights?
- Precisely which skills/compatible weapon sets, materials, and upgrade slots are needed for the earliest solo test?
- How much distance, cover and movement simulation is needed to prove the environmental combat idea without unnecessary complexity?
- How do we ensure characters never suffer irreversible build-choice mistakes while still encouraging two-skill specializations?
- How do we validate equivalent online, speed-up, skip and long-term offline outcomes?

## Explicitly deferred
- NPC party filling, group size, buff economics, clans and multiplayer.
- Character personality and ethics earned by quest actions, later affecting automatic dialogue. **Far-future idea, not a first-release feature.**
- Expanded races, large bestiary, quests, locations and other world systems.

## Parking lot for future discussions

These are **questions, not tasks**:
- Should a mine or hunting zone have procedural events beyond its core resource/enemy?
- What exactly changes a player from “too weak to hunt” to “ready to hunt”?
- Are rare discoveries mostly materials, unique cosmetic artwork/events, permanent unlocks or gear?
- How should multi-year returns be summarized without showing millions of log entries?
- How do we prove that solo/group outcomes and online/offline outcomes are balanced?
- What minimal device/server test would demonstrate the game is lightweight on budget Android phones?
- What should happen if a group dissolves while members are offline?
- Would guilds meaningfully improve the game, or should they wait until later?

## Notes on governance

This document can grow freely. **No item here, including one labeled Direction, is a coding instruction.** Owner approval is required before moving an idea into the durable decision log or an approved implementation task. Keep speculative future features out of `docs/TD_LIST.md` until chosen.

**Working philosophy:** Start with beautiful stone mining and bunny hunting. Make that work reliably, offline forever, on a phone, with the simplest possible calculations. Expand only when the result is fun.
