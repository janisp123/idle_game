Early prototype. Activities repeat automatically. Each activity earns its own skill XP; higher skill can reveal rarer resources without changing activities. New skill levels activate on return. Smelting, alchemy, carpentry, masonry and cooking consume available, unprotected raw materials. Detailed combat is not yet simulated.
